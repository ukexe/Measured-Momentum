# Design System: Measured Momentum

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Kinetic Contact Sheet"**

Measured Momentum is a dark theatrical sports editorial built from photographic
emulsion, contact-sheet registration, and visible evidence. The shipped system
turns the energy of Acrobatic Rock ’n’ Roll and Boogie Woogie into a persuasive
sequence without presenting the proposal as an operating federation or
programme. It records the finished transformation from concept seed `d8310e56`;
the built artifact, including its final homepage review image, is the authority.

The world is premium because it is controlled rather than glossy. Warm-ivory
type sits on near-black stages; supplied rehearsal photography arrives as
full-bleed or deliberately offset plates; lime, pink, teal, indigo, and brass
behave like registration inks, count marks, and evidence signals. Fine rules,
crop marks, eight-count rails, and source-like captions provide structure.
Controls are solid typographic objects. Essential meaning remains static and
readable while Saath Rekha contributes only deferred, nonessential depth.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Near-black photographic emulsion with warm-ivory type.
- Lime, pink, and teal registration marks, with indigo links and brass evidence cues.
- Twelve-column editorial fields and recurring eight-count measurement rails.
- Full-bleed or offset supplied performance plates with provenance captions.
- Static-first meaning with deferred, decorative Saath Rekha depth.

## Colors

The palette behaves like a restrained set of inks printed over a deep stage:
ivory carries the reading load and the bright colors identify beats, balance,
registration, links, and evidence rather than filling every surface.

### Primary

- **Registration Lime:** The most forceful signal. Use it for the mandatory
  proposal-status rail, primary actions, active count marks, focus, and sparse
  registration corners.

### Secondary

- **Beat Pink:** Marks momentum, headline punctuation, discipline energy, and
  error emphasis.
- **Balance Teal:** Marks partnership, secondary action states, and balancing
  registration lines.

### Tertiary

- **Step Indigo:** Carries editorial links and secondary route emphasis.
- **Evidence Brass:** Distinguishes held records, cautions, and evidence-led
  annotations.

### Neutral

- **Stage Black:** The deepest photographic field and full-bleed frame.
- **Stage Ink:** The principal page surface and dark text on bright accents.
- **Stage Slate:** The inset field and form-control surface.
- **Stage Raised:** A restrained higher tonal layer for focused controls and
  selected reading surfaces.
- **Strong Ivory:** Headlines and highest-priority copy.
- **Body Ivory:** Default reading text.
- **Muted Ivory:** Captions, caveats, and secondary copy.
- **Soft and Strong Hairlines:** Registration grids, dividers, frame edges,
  and measured boundaries.

### Named Rules

**The Registration Ink Rule.** Bright colors appear as status fields, type
accents, count marks, crop marks, rules, and bounded action slabs; they are not
ambient decoration.

**The Truthful Status Rule.** Registration Lime may make the proposal status
impossible to miss, but color never replaces the exact “Proposed organisation ·
In development” wording.

**The Accessible Pairing Rule.** State, validation, discipline, and selection
always retain a textual, structural, or shape cue in addition to color.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed, self-hosted, with Arial Narrow and Segoe UI
Variable Display fallbacks.

**Body Font:** Segoe UI Variable Text with Segoe UI, SF Pro Text, Roboto,
Helvetica Neue, and Arial fallbacks.

**Label/Mono Font:** The platform monospace stack led by ui-monospace and
Cascadia Code.

**Character:** Compressed, arena-scale display type supplies athletic force;
neutral system body type keeps institutional caveats legible. Monospaced labels
make status, measurements, counts, and image provenance read like production
records rather than promotional badges.

Only the 600 and 800 Barlow Condensed faces are shipped. Display text therefore
uses those real faces; intermediary weights in older declarations resolve to
the nearest available file rather than defining additional type tokens.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (800, responsive 3.75–6rem, 0.86 line-height): Homepage thesis
  lines and the largest persuasive statement.
- **Headline** (800, responsive 2.75–5.75rem, 0.94 line-height): Major section
  entries and large editorial transitions.
- **Title** (800, responsive 1.375–2rem, 1.1 line-height): Plate titles,
  evidence headings, and compact section statements.
- **Body** (400, 1.125rem, 1.65 line-height): Editorial reading copy, held near
  a 68-character measure.
- **Label** (700–800, 0.6875–0.75rem, 0.11–0.16em tracking, uppercase):
  Registration rails, navigation, counts, status, and provenance.

### Named Rules

**The Condensed Arena Rule.** Use Barlow Condensed for headlines and emphatic
numerals, not for long-form body copy.

**The Metadata Is Evidence Rule.** Monospaced uppercase text is reserved for
real status, count, navigation, measurement, and source information; never use
it to invent an ornamental kicker.

## Layout

The core canvas is a 76rem content container with gutters that scale from 1rem
to 2rem; a 90rem wide container is available for broader plates. Persuasive
surfaces use twelve-column fields, while registration grids and rails divide
space into eight measured beats. One-pixel lines expose alignment rather than
hiding it inside cards.

The shipped first viewport is an editorial spread, not a centered landing-page
stack: a supplied monochrome rehearsal photograph fills the stage, a
high-contrast status caption sits above the copy, the display statement occupies
the left field, and the proposition, non-claim, and actions occupy the right.
The image caption and eight-count rail close the frame. Later sections alternate
full-width plates, offset crops, ledgers, and ruled rows.

Desktop navigation yields to the mobile panel at 75rem. Wide shell structures
compact at 64rem; the primary editorial grid stacks at 56rem; route headers
stack at 44rem; actions and dense rows become single-column by 36rem. At every
breakpoint, status and copy precede decorative media, the page reflows to 320px,
and touch targets retain a practical minimum of 2.75rem.

**The Measured Field Rule.** Align persuasive surfaces to twelve columns and
rhythmic metadata to eight counts; asymmetry is deliberate, but edges always
register.

**The Photograph Is a Plate Rule.** Performance imagery is full-bleed or
staggered and receives a source caption; it is never presented as an anonymous
generic card gallery.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is mostly flat and ruled. Depth comes from photographic exposure,
overlap, dark tonal layers, restrained image washes, and a small vocabulary of
soft black shadows under physical plates and primary controls. Registration
lines stay crisp and unshadowed. Translucency may support a necessary overlay,
but decorative glass is not the defining material.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Photographic Plate** (`0 2rem 5rem rgb(0 0 0 / 0.32)`): Separates a
  performance frame from the stage without making it a floating card.
- **Primary Control** (`0.65rem 0.7rem 1.7rem rgb(0 0 0 / 0.28)`): Gives the
  solid lime action a physical printed-object quality.
- **Primary Control Hover** (`0.65rem 0.85rem 2rem rgb(0 0 0 / 0.38)`):
  Deepens the same shadow while the control rises by 0.125rem.
- **Editorial Float** (`0.9rem 1.6rem 4.5rem rgb(0 0 0 / 0.46)`): Reserved for
  substantial overlays and legacy reading surfaces, not routine containers.

### Named Rules

**The Plate, Not Card Rule.** Use overlap, crop, contrast, and one purposeful
shadow to establish depth; do not wrap every idea in a glowing elevated card.

**The Fallback First Rule.** The photograph, HTML, and CSS composition must be
complete before Saath Rekha loads; removing WebGL cannot remove meaning.

## Shapes

The dominant form is rectilinear: square photographic frames, square status
captions, hard-edged buttons, ruled ledgers, and narrow registration corners.
Controls may use a 0.125rem corner and small containers a 0.25rem corner, but
large soft radii are not part of the finished contact-sheet language. One- and
two-pixel borders do most of the separating.

Circles and ellipses are semantic exceptions for focus reticles, count rhythm,
the paired-ribbon mark, and Saath Rekha geometry. They do not become a generic
badge or container shape.

**The Registration Edge Rule.** Default to a square edge; round only when the
shape expresses focus, orbit, partnership, or a native control affordance.

## Components

### Buttons

Buttons are compact printed labels: uppercase monospace text, hard edges, and a
3rem minimum height.

- **Shape:** Square by default, with 0.9rem × 1.25rem internal padding.
- **Primary:** Registration Lime with Stage Ink text and a restrained offset
  black shadow.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover moves up 0.125rem and brightens to the shipped pale
  lime; focus uses a 3px Registration Lime outline, a 3px offset, and a Stage
  Black separation ring. Active returns to the baseline.
- **Secondary:** A dark translucent stage field with a strong hairline; hover
  becomes Balance Teal with Stage Ink text.
- **Quiet:** Transparent with a strong hairline; hover changes the border and
  label to Registration Lime.
- **Disabled:** Stage Raised, muted text, no lift or shadow, and an explicit
  disabled state.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** Stage Slate fill, Strong Ivory text, a 0.125rem corner, a visible
  light border, and a 3rem minimum height.
- **Focus:** Registration Lime border and outline with a restrained lime halo.
- **Error / Disabled:** Beat Pink border plus written error copy; unavailable
  submission behavior remains explicit and fail-closed.

### Navigation

Desktop navigation uses uppercase monospace labels on a ruled black header.
Default links are Body Ivory; hover and open states shift to Registration Lime
and expose a thin registration underline. The primary “Register interest”
route is a solid lime rectangle. At 75rem, navigation becomes a full-height
dark panel with large Barlow Condensed route labels, visible current-page
structure, and the proposal statement retained.

### Status Rail

The global rail is a full-width Registration Lime field with Stage Ink
monospace text. It always contains the exact mandatory status and a visible
“What that means” link. Its square outline marker is a registration device, not
a decorative dot.

### Page Header

Route headers pair an oversized condensed title and a teal-topped description
with a vertical eight-count measure. The measure disappears when the layout
stacks; the title and description remain complete.

### Performance Plate

Supplied photography sits inside a square Stage Black frame with a strong
hairline, opposing lime crop corners, an “MM” registration index, and an
optional focus reticle. Captions are monospaced and split into source label and
truthful context. Monochrome is a supported treatment; hover changes exposure
slightly but never supplies meaning.

### Evidence Ledger

Evidence is rendered as ruled definition-list rows, not promotional statistics.
Subjects use muted monospaced labels; states use Registration Lime display type;
details remain Body Ivory. Missing evidence is shown as material absence rather
than replaced with sample content.

### Saath Rekha

Saath Rekha is decorative paired-ribbon and registration depth. Its static CSS
and SVG fallback is immediate; Three/R3F is deferred, demand-rendered, capped
for device capability, and absent under reduced motion, forced colors,
Save-Data, unsupported WebGL, or failure. It never carries instructions,
identity, authority, gender, nationality, or institutional meaning.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep “Proposed organisation · In development” visibly present in the
  global shell and pair visual status with exact explanatory language.
- **Do** use self-hosted Barlow Condensed for high-impact display roles and
  system fonts for readable editorial copy.
- **Do** treat supplied performance images as illustrative plates with truthful
  provenance and non-claim captions.
- **Do** use lime, pink, teal, indigo, and brass as measured registration inks,
  preserving Strong or Body Ivory for sustained reading.
- **Do** keep essential information in semantic HTML and preserve static,
  reduced-motion, forced-colors, print, and no-WebGL fallbacks.
- **Do** keep editorial routes static-export compatible, trailing-slashed, and
  independent of request-time page data.

### Don't:

- **Don't** imply legal status, recognition, affiliation, authority, people,
  results, events, sponsors, official contact details, or operating services
  without verified evidence and publication approval.
- **Don't** turn the world into a generic dark sports landing page made from a
  centered slogan, decorative glass cards, anonymous statistics, or repeated
  ambient glows.
- **Don't** use invented kickers, glyph icons, or monospaced labels as empty
  decoration; every metadata treatment must carry real status, count,
  navigation, measurement, or source information.
- **Don't** make Saath Rekha essential, immediate, motion-dependent, or more
  visually authoritative than the proposal and its evidence.
- **Don't** return visual or copy success for an enquiry unless durable,
  server-validated persistence and its controls are configured and verified.
