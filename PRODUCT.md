# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The public website serves newcomers, athletes and guardians, coaches and
judges, contributors, researchers, media, institutions, and readers evaluating
the credibility of a proposed Indian initiative for Acrobatic Rock ’n’ Roll and
Boogie Woogie.

## Product Purpose

Measured Momentum explains the two disciplines, shows how a responsible
initiative might develop, and makes the current evidence and governance limits
easy to inspect. Success means a first-time visitor understands the disciplines,
the proposal’s ambition, its non-claims, and the safest next route without
mistaking the website for an operating federation or programme.

## Positioning

The product combines performance energy with unusually explicit institutional
boundaries: momentum is visible, but every claim, empty state, and future
pathway remains inspectable and conditional.

## Operating Context

Visitors primarily read public editorial pages on mobile and desktop. They may
compare disciplines, inspect governance status, review proposed pathways, or
check whether an enquiry capability is responsibly available. Editorial
content is repository-owned and statically exported.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, static export, and trailing
  slashes are fixed architectural commitments.
- Cloudflare Static Assets owns non-API delivery; only `/api/*` is Worker-first.
- Enquiries remain fail-closed until durable persistence and operational
  controls are configured and verified.
- Server Components and semantic HTML are the default; client islands stay
  narrow.
- Public people, results, events, affiliations, recognition, authority,
  sponsors, and contact details require verified evidence and publication
  approval.
- Empty people, competition, and news collections are intentional.

## Brand Commitments

- Creative platform: Measured Momentum.
- Working descriptor: Acrobatic Rock ’n’ Roll + Boogie Woogie in India.
- Mandatory status: Proposed organisation · In development.
- Preserve the dark theatrical identity and its coral, teal, lime, indigo,
  brass, ivory, and near-black palette.
- Saath Rekha is a decorative, fallback-first motion system, not a public
  organisation name or a source of essential meaning.

## Evidence on Hand

- Typed public content and publication gates under `content/`.
- Canonical governance and evidence rules under `docs/`.
- Preserved research/design evidence under `docs/drafts/`, which is not public
  copy.
- Fifteen supplied performance and training photographs under
  `womensformation/`. They may be used as illustrative imagery only; no
  identity, result, representation, event relationship, or proposal membership
  may be inferred from them.
- No verified founder roster, official team, event archive, competition result,
  sponsor, legal identity, or public contact exists.

## Product Principles

1. Truth before scale.
2. Performance energy without institutional overclaim.
3. Welfare and consent before progression or visibility.
4. Make status, evidence, and absent capabilities explicit.
5. Keep essential information independent of JavaScript and WebGL.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic structure, keyboard access, visible
focus, reduced-motion and forced-colours behavior, 320px reflow, readable
editorial measures, and meaningful static fallbacks.
