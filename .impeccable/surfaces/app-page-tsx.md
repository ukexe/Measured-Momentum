# Homepage and global shell

Mode: Persuade, with Read behavior for governance and legal surfaces.

Audience: people encountering the two disciplines or evaluating whether this
proposal is credible. The immediate action is to understand the proposal, then
choose the relevant information or interest route without mistaking interest
for membership.

Content proof: the real supplied performance/training photography, discipline
orientation, explicit status ledger, truthful empty states, and source-led
governance boundaries. Supplied images remain illustrative and establish no
identity, result, representation, or relationship.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Build a kinetic contact sheet: one continuous editorial sequence where
performance photography, motion traces, and evidence captions make energy and
accountability visible together. Refuse the generic dark sports landing page
of a centred slogan, glowing cards, and anonymous stat blocks.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black photographic emulsion, warm-ivory type, and the existing
coral, teal, lime, indigo, and brass inks. Photography arrives as decisive
full-bleed plates or staggered frames, never a generic card gallery. Fine
registration rules, crop marks, count rails, and source-like captions replace
decorative glass. Controls are solid typographic objects with precise motion.

STORY: The visitor first experiences athletic partnership, then understands
the two disciplines, the proposed development logic, the withheld claims, and
the governance boundary. The close routes each audience to an honest next
step. No visual treatment turns a supplied subject into an official person or
turns a proposal into an operating service.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A monochrome training photograph fills the viewport and leaves
its natural black negative space for the headline. The status is a high-contrast
live caption at the top, the headline spans the left and centre at arena scale,
the non-claim and actions anchor the lower edge, and Saath Rekha passes behind
the photograph as restrained chromatic movement. The next photographic plate
is visible at the fold. The signature interaction is a slow contact-sheet
registration shift: image crop, count line, and 3D rhythm move at different
bounded rates while content remains still and readable.

FORM: Kinetic Contact Sheet, the assigned third grounded direction from seed
`d8310e56`. It carries the incumbent dark-stage identity forward through
photographic emulsion and editorial evidence rather than adding another visual
theme.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance

Constraints: static export, trailing slashes, Server Components by default,
one deferred global WebGL context, reduced-motion/forced-colours/Save-Data
fallback, no invented people or institutional claims, legal pages optimized
for reading, and all enquiry behavior unchanged and fail-closed.
