# Affiliation questions

**Canonical status:** unresolved institutional-status gate  
**Research cutoff:** 25 October 2026  
**Detailed question set:**
[`drafts/affiliation-questions.md`](drafts/affiliation-questions.md)

Use the detailed record for the complete, numbered WRRC and WDSF request. This
summary identifies the minimum answers needed before any public membership,
affiliation, recognition, representation, sanction or logo claim.

## Known record and exact distinctions

- WRRC lists **Acrobatic Rock'n'Roll Federation of India** as
  **“On Hold.”** The reviewed statutes do not define that label.
- WDSF separately lists **Amateur DanceSport Federation of India (ADSFI)** as
  **“Full”** and **“Recognised by NOC: No.”**
- WDSF lists WRRC as an **Associate** member.

These are three distinct directory facts. They do not establish that ARRFI and
ADSFI are the same body or related, that a WRRC national listing receives WDSF
status, or that either Indian listing has any identity or relationship with
this proposal. “Full” must not be rewritten as Indian government or NOC
recognition; “On Hold” must not be interpreted without WRRC’s written
definition.

## Questions that must be resolved

Request a joint or mutually consistent documentary answer covering:

1. the exact registered legal identity and current authority behind WRRC’s
   India entry;
2. the formal meaning, start date, basis, rights and restrictions of
   “On Hold”;
3. whether that entry continues to occupy WRRC’s one-country position and the
   permitted route for a new Indian group;
4. any agreement, delegation, affiliation or other relationship between the
   WRRC India entry and ADSFI;
5. how WRRC’s WDSF Associate status affects, or does not affect, WRRC national
   members;
6. which entity, if any, may currently register Indian participants, sanction
   events, use national-representation wording or administer these disciplines;
7. whether this proposal may truthfully describe discussions, an application
   path or another limited status, and the exact approved sentence;
8. separate written permission for every WRRC/WDSF logo, badge or brand use;
9. canonical records for each founder result, licence, qualification or role;
10. the revalidation date and authorised institutional contact for every
    answer.

Indian legal status is a separate question. WRRC or WDSF cannot substitute for
current advice from Indian counsel and the competent Indian authorities on
legal form, name use, designated-sport status, government/NOC recognition or
the provisions in force.

## Evidence protocol

- Send requests from a retained organisational mailbox and copy the relevant
  formal office.
- Ask the respondent to state their capacity and decision authority.
- Require the exact legal entity, status/class, discipline, territory,
  effective date, conditions, rights, restrictions and source instrument.
- Preserve the complete thread and attachments from published institutional
  addresses.
- Treat calls and informal messages as orientation only.
- Address status, athlete/event authority and logo rights separately; one does
  not prove the others.

## Minimum adequate answer

Do not close a record on “yes,” “supported,” “known to us,” “under process” or
“no objection.” A publishable answer must:

- name the exact Indian legal entity and authorised issuing officer;
- identify the governing instrument and decision-maker;
- state status/class, disciplines, territory, rights and restrictions;
- give effective, review and expiry dates;
- address ARRFI, ADSFI and this proposal separately;
- explain participant registration and event authority separately;
- authorise the exact website wording;
- grant or deny logo use separately;
- provide a current revalidation contact.

## Publication gate

Until adequate written answers and the matching
[`content-verification.md`](content-verification.md) records are approved, the
website may state only that this is a proposed organisation and that no
affiliation, recognition or governing-body status is claimed.

Do not publish “WRRC member,” “WDSF affiliated,” “under WRRC/WDSF,” “official
Indian body,” “represents India,” “national federation,” “successor,”
“chapter,” “partner,” “sanctioned” or similar wording. Do not display
WRRC/WDSF marks.

Legal/privacy/terms wording, the public name and any final outreach position
require professional Indian review. Verified operator identity, founder
claims, contact details, domain, institutional answers, logo/photo rights,
Cloudflare/Supabase ownership and live RLS evidence remain external release
blockers.
