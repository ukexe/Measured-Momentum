This document is not legal advice. Indian counsel and the relevant sports bodies should review any name, status, affiliation, logo or representation claim before publication.

## How to read this draft

- **Verified fact** means the cited official source says the stated thing.
- **Cautious interpretation** means a limited conclusion from the verified record; it is not a legal determination.
- **Unresolved question** means the public record reviewed does not answer the point, or the answer requires a document or written confirmation.
- “ARRFI” below is only a report shorthand for the entity named by WRRC; the WRRC directory does not publish an abbreviation for it.

## 3. WRRC membership requirements found in official sources

**Verified fact.** WRRC’s current Statutes say:

- national Rock ’n’ Roll federations whose statutes commit them to promoting and furthering Rock ’n’ Roll dance, including compliant acrobatic creations, may become ordinary members;
- an application goes in writing to the WRRC office with the applicant’s statutes, member list and managerial-committee list;
- the applicant’s statutes and regulations must accord with the spirit of the WRRC Statutes;
- the General Meeting decides admission by simple majority;
- a new member receives provisional membership for three years, with the current text also providing for one renewal and later ordinary membership;
- the Presidium may decide temporary membership between General Meetings;
- only one national federation per country may be admitted; it must represent the country’s non-profit sports movement; and, wherever possible, membership of the national sport federation or National Olympic Committee is required.

Source — *STATUTES WRRC*, §§4–5: https://www.wrrc.org/documents/general/AR_0001_14_Statutes.pdf (accessed 25 October 2026).

## 6. Public-language controls

### Do not publish without exact documentary support

- “the national federation,” “India’s federation,” “National Sports Federation,” or “national governing body”;
- “official,” “authorised,” “approved,” “recognised,” “accredited,” “member” or “affiliate” without naming the granting body, scope, status and date;
- “represents India,” “Team India,” “Indian national team,” “national championship,” or “selects India’s athletes”;
- “under WRRC/WDSF,” “WRRC India,” “WDSF India,” “partner of,” or “in association with”;
- “successor to,” “revival of,” “chapter of,” or “working through” ARRFI or ADSFI;
- any claim that “On Hold” means active, suspended, terminated or restorable;
- any founder title, international participation, qualification, placement or award still marked unverified in the content-verification register.

### Safer interim wording

- “a proposed Indian organisation focused on developing Acrobatic Rock ’n’ Roll and Boogie Woogie”;
- “an independent initiative seeking written clarification on the recognised pathway for the disciplines in India”;
- “the founders plan to support training, community participation and properly sanctioned competition opportunities”;
- “no affiliation, recognition or governing-body status is claimed unless and until confirmed in writing.”

### Term-by-term rule

- **Federation:** reserve for an incorporated body whose constitution, membership base and representative authority are verified. In this context the word can imply national representative authority.
- **National / India / Indian:** do not use to imply exclusivity, government status or authority to represent India. The enacted Act contains a section 27 approval regime, but that section was not found in the commencement notices reviewed; obtain current Indian legal advice rather than claiming either that approval is already legally required under that section or that use is unrestricted. Sources — enacted §27: https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/265482.pdf; commencement notices: https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/268971.pdf, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/272555.pdf, and https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/275571.pdf (all accessed 25 October 2026).
- **Recognised:** always say “recognised by [named body] as [status] from [date],” and retain the underlying instrument.
- **Affiliated:** identify both parties, the instrument, effective date, scope, conditions and current standing.
- **Official:** use only for an official document, role or event where the appointing/sanctioning body can be named.
- **Governing body:** use only where a current constitutive, statutory or recognition instrument grants that jurisdiction.
