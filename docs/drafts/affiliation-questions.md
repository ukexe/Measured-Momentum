# Affiliation and status questions

**Purpose:** obtain documentary answers before the proposed organisation uses any federation, national, official, recognised, governing-body, membership, affiliation, representation or logo claim.  
**Cut-off:** 25 October 2026  
**Important:** send these from a retained organisational mailbox, request a reply from the recipient’s published institutional address, and save the complete thread and attachments. An informal call or message is useful for orientation but is not sufficient publication evidence. This is not legal advice.

## 2. Cover note to use with every request

> We are forming a proposed Indian organisation focused on Acrobatic Rock ’n’ Roll and Boogie Woogie. We are not claiming membership, affiliation, recognition, governing authority or a right to represent India. Before publishing a website or accepting members, athletes or event entries, we seek a written, document-backed clarification of the permitted institutional route. Please identify the exact legal entity to which each answer applies, the decision-maker, the effective date, the current status, any conditions, and the source document or rule. Where an answer is outside your authority, please refer it to the authorised person rather than treating the question as agreed.

## 8. Minimum adequate answer

Do not close the affiliation question merely because a respondent says “yes,” “supported,” “known to us,” “under process” or “no objection.” A publishable answer should:

1. name the exact Indian legal entity;
2. identify the issuing body and authorised signatory;
3. identify the status/class and disciplines;
4. state the territory and rights;
5. state restrictions, conditions and good-standing position;
6. state effective and review/expiry dates;
7. identify the governing instrument and decision;
8. address ARRFI, ADSFI and the proposed organisation separately;
9. authorise the exact public wording;
10. address logo use separately from status;
11. explain athlete/event authority separately from membership;
12. provide a revalidation contact.

Until that package exists, the website should say only that the organisation is proposed and is seeking formal clarification; it should not claim an affiliation or national governing role.
