# Content-verification register

**Draft status:** publication gate for the proposed website  
**Cut-off:** 25 October 2026  
**Non-negotiable starting position:** **all founder details are unverified.** No founder biography, role, title, competition, placement, qualification, photo caption or quotation may be published until the relevant item below is closed.

This register verifies claims; it does not confer legal, sporting or institutional status. It is not legal advice.

## Status codes and publication rule

- **U — Unverified:** no adequate evidence; do not publish.
- **P — Partially verified:** some evidence exists but an essential element is missing; do not publish the broad claim. A narrower, accurately sourced statement may be approved.
- **V — Verified:** the exact wording is supported by primary evidence and has been approved for the stated channel and review period.
- **D — Disputed:** conflicting records or an objection exists; do not publish without a resolved record and senior/legal sign-off.
- **E — Expired:** evidence was once adequate but is no longer current; remove or reverify.
- **N/A — Not used:** the claim will not appear.

For status **V**, retain: the exact approved sentence; source file/URL; issuer; event or instrument date; access/verification date; verifier; rights/consent evidence; review date; and a screenshot or archived copy where lawful.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Official registry extract, Gazette instrument, signed result protocol, official membership profile, licence register, governing-body letter or qualification-verification response.
2. Direct confirmation from the issuing body’s published institutional domain, naming the person/entity, scope, status and effective date.
3. Contemporaneous corroboration such as entry credentials, programmes, travel records, photos or press coverage.
4. Self-authored CVs, social posts, posters and unsourced certificates.

Tier 3 and 4 material may corroborate a claim but should not independently establish legal status, affiliation, representation of India, a title, placement or qualification.

## Register

### ORG-001 — Proposed organisation identity

- **Status:** U — blocked.
- **Claim controlled:** exact public name, abbreviations, former names and domain/social handles.
- **Required evidence:** name-availability search; founders’ approved name resolution; legal review of “Federation,” “National,” “India” and “Indian”; trademark search; and, after registration, a current registry extract.
- **Acceptance test:** every character matches the registration and approved brand record; no confusing or unauthorised affiliation implication.
- **Public rule:** until closed, use “the proposed organisation” and do not present a final legal name.

### ORG-002 — Organisational status and scope

- **Status:** U — blocked.
- **Claim controlled:** non-profit, national, official, federation, governing body, representative body, recognised body, membership body or sports promotion organisation.
- **Required evidence:** constitutive document, legal registration, membership structure, territorial scope, governing-body instrument and any Ministry/NOC/international recognition.
- **Public rule:** aspirations may be described as plans; existing authority may not be implied.

## Release checklist

Before a website release, the content owner must:

1. list every factual sentence and link it to one or more register IDs;
2. confirm that each published claim is V, not U/P/D/E;
3. copy the exact approved wording rather than paraphrasing status;
4. re-open every current-status URL on the release date;
5. verify that logo licences and photo consents cover the intended channel;
6. have Indian counsel review name, representation, recognition and fundraising claims;
7. record reviewer, approval date and next review date;
8. remove or correct any claim immediately if a source changes or an issuing body objects.
