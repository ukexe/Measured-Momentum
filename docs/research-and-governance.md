# Research and governance

**Canonical status:** publication summary and governance gate  
**Research cutoff and source access date:** 25 October 2026  
**Scope:** proposed Indian organisation focused on Acrobatic Rock ’n’ Roll and
Boogie Woogie

The detailed source review is preserved in
[`drafts/research-and-governance.md`](drafts/research-and-governance.md). That
record is evidence, not approved public copy. This summary does not provide
legal advice and must be revalidated before a release that relies on current
status.

## Findings that may be stated exactly

- WRRC’s members directory lists **Acrobatic Rock'n'Roll Federation of India**
  for India with the exact status **“On Hold.”** The reviewed WRRC statutes do
  not define that directory label. Do not paraphrase it as active, suspended,
  inactive, terminated, in good standing or carrying full rights.
- WDSF separately lists **Amateur DanceSport Federation of India (ADSFI)** with
  status **“Full”**, full member since **20 June 2007**, and
  **“Recognised by NOC: No.”** “Full” is a WDSF membership class; it is not
  evidence of Indian government or NOC recognition.
- WDSF lists WRRC itself as an **Associate** member. That international
  relationship does not establish WDSF membership for each WRRC national
  listing.
- The names, cities and contacts on the WRRC and WDSF India records differ, and
  neither profile cross-references the other.
- The reviewed Ministry 2026 recognition page displayed no ARRFI, ADSFI or
  DanceSport entry. The defensible statement is only that **no recognition
  record was located in the official materials reviewed**; absence from that
  search is not proof of no historic, pending, differently named or
  non-indexed record.
- The National Sports Governance Act, 2025 was only partly commenced at the
  cutoff. The three located commencement notifications did not verify
  commencement of the main section 8 application machinery or section 27.
  Four related 2026 rule instruments were located. Current Indian counsel and
  the Ministry must confirm the law and process in force at publication.

Principal official sources and the full legal/governance analysis are linked
in sections 1–5 and 10 of the detailed record.

## What the evidence does not establish

The reviewed records do not establish:

- that ARRFI and ADSFI are the same legal person, affiliates, successors,
  parent/subsidiary bodies or authorised representatives of one another;
- any identity, affiliation, agency, delegation, recognition or other
  relationship between either listed entity and this proposal or its founders;
- the legal registration, current office-bearers or authority of either
  directory entry;
- why the WRRC India entry is “On Hold,” when that status began, its legal or
  sporting consequences, or a route out of it;
- a right for this proposal to represent India, select or register athletes,
  sanction events, issue titles, use WRRC/WDSF marks or call itself a national
  federation or governing body;
- founder biographies, roles, international participation, qualifications,
  placements or awards.

No identity, relationship or recognition may be inferred through a chain such
as “WRRC is a WDSF Associate, therefore a WRRC India listing is WDSF
recognised.” Each claimed relationship needs its own current instrument.

## Publication position

Until the relevant verification records close, public copy may describe only:

> a proposed Indian organisation focused on developing Acrobatic Rock ’n’
> Roll and Boogie Woogie; no affiliation, recognition or governing-body status
> is claimed.

Do not publish “federation,” “national governing body,” “official,”
“recognised,” “affiliated,” “WRRC India,” “WDSF India,” “represents India,”
“Team India,” “national championship,” “successor,” “chapter,” or comparable
authority wording without a dated primary instrument, exact approved wording
and publication approval.

## Governance gate

Before the proposal can be presented as an operating organisation, retain and
approve:

1. the exact legal name, form, registry evidence and accountable operator;
2. adopted governing documents, decision rights, office-bearer authority,
   conflicts and terms;
3. India-specific legal advice on the name, activities and law in force;
4. written WRRC/WDSF/ADSFI/Ministry/NOC determinations where any such status is
   claimed;
5. safeguarding, complaints, privacy, retention, financial and incident
   processes with named operational owners;
6. publication evidence for every person, founder claim, event, result,
   contact, logo and image.

Privacy notices, terms, consent text and other legal/policy content remain
**DRAFT — REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL LEGAL REVIEW; NOT LEGAL ADVICE** until the
verified operator and a qualified Indian professional approve them.
